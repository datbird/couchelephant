Set up CouchElephant

Point it at your Plex server. That is the only thing it needs to start.
The address has to work from inside this container. If Plex runs on another machine, use that machine's address, not 127.0.0.1.
On the Plex server this is the PlexOnlineToken in Preferences.xml. It is kept in the local database and never logged.
Guide times come from Plex in this zone, and every time here is shown in it. Pick where the server is, not where you are, if they differ.

Two things you can leave for later

Neither is needed to start, and both live in Settings once you are in.

Sign-in Nobody is asked to sign in. That suits a box on your own network. Under Settings, Accounts you can switch to accounts kept here, or hand the question to Cloudflare Access, before this is reachable from anywhere else.
Preview mode On to start with, so nothing is written to your DVR until you have seen CouchElephant choose correctly. Turn it off under Settings, Recording.
